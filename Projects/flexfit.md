### Flexfit
🔗 **Live site:** [flexfit.com](https://www.flexfit.com/)

**Role:** Front-end development & site maintenance · **Platform:** Webflow (e-commerce) · **Timeline:** [Ongoing / dates]

An e-commerce site for Flexfit, a headwear brand with a large and frequently
changing product catalog. I maintained and updated the live site, keeping
product information, branding, and seasonal campaigns accurate across every
page, while making sure changes didn't break existing layouts or navigation.

![Flexfit homepage](assets/flexfit/homepage.png)

#### Highlights

**Site-wide product name updates**
Rolled out product name changes across the entire site, updating every page,
product listing, and reference so naming stayed consistent everywhere
customers might see it.

![Product page](assets/flexfit/product-page.png)

**Trademark compliance**
Manually added trademark symbols to product names across the site's pages to
keep the brand's legal and branding requirements consistent.

**Holiday and product launch campaigns**
- Updated homepage banners for holidays and seasonal promotions
- Refreshed specific product pages to support new product launches
- Kept campaign messaging and visuals consistent across the site

![Campaign banner](assets/flexfit/campaign-banner.png)

**Page cleanup and redirects**
- Removed outdated pages from the site
- Set up redirects from old URLs to their new pages so visitors and search
  engines never land on a broken link

**Tech:** Webflow · HTML · CSS · CMS · E-commerce · Redirect management
